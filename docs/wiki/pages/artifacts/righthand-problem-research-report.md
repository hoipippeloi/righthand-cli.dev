## What it documents

- The **five failure modes** coding agents hit when operational tasks enter the loop — see [[operational-task-failure-modes-of-coding-agents]].
- The **competitive landscape** — MCP servers (saturated, host-bound) vs raw CLIs like `gh`/`kubectl` (universal but context-expensive).

- Two strategic watch-outs: compress-don't-relay ([[compress-don-t-relay]]) and plugin-contract-is-the-moat ([[extensible-plugin-system-is-a-first-class-architectural-pill]]).
